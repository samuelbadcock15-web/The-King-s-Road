🏰 TERRITORY & BUILD RULE:
• Complete all 4 walls around your starting base first!

📱 MOBILE CONTROLS:
• Select Unit: Tap a troop
• Move Unit: Tap destination on the ground
• Camera Pan: Use left or right D-Pad
• Deselect: Tap the glowing [DESELECT] button

💻 PC CONTROLS:
• Select & Move: Click unit, then click destination
• Camera Move: WASD keys
• Deselect: Click [DESELECT] on the left sidebar
